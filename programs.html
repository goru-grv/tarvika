Digital Marketing

Audience insight, content and measurable digital campaigns.

Explore how organizations reach and serve audiences online. This learning area brings together strategy, creative communication and measurement, helping participants understand how digital channels support a brand or mission.

Possible learning areas

  • Digital strategy and audience research
  • Content planning and brand storytelling
  • Search and social media fundamentals
  • Campaign measurement and analytics
  • Ethical marketing and privacy awareness

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Data, intelligent systems and responsible AI practice.

Build an understanding of intelligent systems, the data they use and the methods that help them learn. Explore practical applications alongside evaluation, limitations and human-centred AI considerations.

Possible learning areas

  • AI and machine learning foundations
  • Data preparation and exploration
  • Model training and evaluation
  • Applied AI use cases
  • Responsible AI, safety and ethics

Agri Tech

Digital tools and data for agriculture and food systems.

Explore how technology can support agricultural decisions, resource awareness and innovation across farming and food systems. Consider practical constraints and farmer-centred design alongside new tools.

Possible learning areas

  • Agriculture technology landscape
  • Sensors, IoT and field data
  • Data-informed crop and resource decisions
  • Precision agriculture concepts
  • Sustainable, farmer-centred innovation

Space Technology

Space systems, satellite data and applications.

Get introduced to technologies and systems behind space exploration and satellite-enabled services. Connect space concepts with applications such as Earth observation and consider responsible, sustainable activity in space.

Possible learning areas

  • Space systems and mission basics
  • Satellites and remote sensing
  • Earth observation applications
  • Space data and emerging technologies
  • Responsible and sustainable space activity

Cyber Security

Foundations in protecting systems, networks and information.

Learn core concepts for protecting information, devices and networks. Develop a security-minded approach to common digital risks and defensive practice, with a strong emphasis on permission, privacy and responsible conduct.

Possible learning areas

  • Security principles and threat awareness
  • Network and system fundamentals
  • Identity, access and data protection
  • Secure practices and risk reduction
  • Ethics and responsible security work

Web Development & DevOps

Build for the web and understand modern delivery.

Explore how modern websites and applications are designed, developed, tested and delivered. Connect frontend and backend foundations with version control, collaboration and reliable deployment workflows.

Possible learning areas

  • Web structure, styling and interactivity
  • Frontend and backend fundamentals
  • Version control and team workflows
  • Testing, deployment and CI/CD concepts
  • Cloud-native and operational foundations

Digital Forensics

Careful examination and preservation of digital evidence.

Explore the methodical process of identifying, preserving and examining digital evidence. Learn why integrity, documentation, privacy and legal context matter throughout an investigation.

Possible learning areas

  • Digital evidence and forensic principles
  • Evidence preservation and chain of custody
  • Storage, filesystems and metadata
  • Forensic examination fundamentals
  • Ethics, privacy and legal awareness
