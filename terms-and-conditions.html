Terms & Conditions

These terms explain how TARVIKA Research and Technology learning, registration, admission enquiries, research proposals, payments and submitted information are handled.

Please read before registering or paying. Program details and fees can vary. The applicable program-specific fee and terms must be shown to you before payment. If a payment page, signed agreement or program notice has a more specific lawful term, that term applies to that transaction where it is consistent with applicable law.

1. About TARVIKA and these terms

TARVIKA Research and Technology is a research, education and technology-focused institution based in Udaipur, Rajasthan. The site describes courses, training, workshops, research-oriented activities and collaboration enquiries. By using this website, creating an account, submitting a form or enrolling in a program, you agree to the terms that apply to that activity.

These terms do not replace a separate written agreement for a specific course, institutional collaboration, research project or service.

2. Programs, eligibility and learning outcomes

Program areas may include Digital Marketing, Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Agri Tech, Space Technology, Cyber Security, Web Development and DevOps, and Digital Forensics. The exact syllabus, instructor, delivery mode, schedule, duration, prerequisites, seat availability and assessment method are program-specific and may change. Confirm current details with TARVIKA before registering or paying.

Learning outcomes depend on the participant's effort, prior knowledge, attendance, access to tools and the specific program. TARVIKA does not guarantee employment, placement, salary, internship, business results, research acceptance or a particular technical outcome.

3. Degrees, certificates and recognition

TARVIKA does not award university degrees, including BTech, BCA or other undergraduate/postgraduate degrees. TARVIKA provides skill training and research-oriented learning activities. A certificate, recommendation letter or other document may be issued only when that specific program offers it and its stated completion criteria are met; issuance is not automatic or guaranteed.

Any certificate or academic recognition must identify the issuing organization and applicable terms. A course certificate or recommendation letter is not a university degree, professional licence or guarantee of admission or employment.

4. Fees, cancellation and refunds

  • Registration fee: The program registration fee will be disclosed before payment and may range from ₹100 to ₹1,000 depending on the program. This registration fee is non-refundable, except where a refund is required by applicable law or TARVIKA confirms otherwise in writing.
  • Course fee cancellation window: If you decide not to continue, send a written cancellation request to info@tarvikart.com within 7 calendar days after completing course registration/payment. The course fee paid is eligible for a refund under this policy; the non-refundable registration fee is excluded.
  • After 7 calendar days: Course fees are non-refundable after the 7-day cancellation window, except where a refund is required by applicable law or TARVIKA confirms otherwise in writing.
  • How to request: Include the learner's name, registered email, program and payment reference. TARVIKA may verify the transaction and will communicate the refund decision and processing steps.
  • Other charges: Any taxes, payment-gateway charges, materials or third-party costs must be identified before payment. No unlisted fee should be assumed.

For clarity, the 7-day course-fee cancellation period does not make the separately disclosed registration fee refundable. If a program is cancelled by TARVIKA, the affected learner will be informed of the available remedy, including any applicable refund.

5. Accounts and account security

Provide accurate details and use an email account you control. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for activity under your account. Do not share your password with staff or other users. TARVIKA will not ask you to send a password by email or form.

Researcher and student portals display information associated with the signed-in account. Tell TARVIKA promptly if you suspect unauthorized account access. Account access may be restricted to protect users, systems or records.

6. Personal information and privacy

TARVIKA collects only the information requested in a particular form, which may include identity and contact details, education history, parent/guardian and emergency contacts, research proposal details, application status and fee records. This information is used to respond to enquiries, process registrations or admission applications, administer learning activities, review proposals, communicate decisions and maintain necessary records.

Account credentials are handled by Firebase Authentication; passwords are not intended to be stored in form records. Text records are handled through Firebase Realtime Database. Admission documents such as photos and PDFs are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket and should be accessible only to the relevant student and authorized administrators. Do not upload information that is unnecessary for the stated form purpose.

TARVIKA should apply reasonable access controls and security practices, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed absolutely secure. Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate the website and services. Retention, correction, deletion and other rights will be handled in accordance with applicable Indian law and operational/legal requirements. For a privacy request, contact info@tarvikart.com.

Personal information is handled subject to applicable Indian law, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules applicable to the information and service, and privacy/data-protection law in force. This page is a general notice and does not replace a legally reviewed privacy notice for a particular service.

7. Uploads and acceptable use

Where an admission portal accepts files, only the displayed file types are allowed and each file must be smaller than 2 MB. Upload genuine, relevant files that you are authorized to share. Do not upload malware, illegal material, another person's private documents without permission, confidential research or unrelated sensitive data.

Do not attempt to access another person's account or records, bypass controls, disrupt services, probe systems without written authorization, or use the site for unlawful, misleading or harmful activity. Security and forensic exercises must remain within an expressly authorized scope.

8. Research proposals and collaboration

A proposal submission is an enquiry for review; it does not establish a partnership, mentorship, funding, publication, acceptance or ownership arrangement. Do not submit confidential, personally identifiable, patient, student, proprietary or security-sensitive data in an initial proposal.

Before research begins, the relevant parties must agree scope, roles, timeline, approvals, data handling, intellectual property, publication/authorship expectations, costs and deliverables in writing. Work involving people, sensitive data or regulated settings must have appropriate permissions and ethics approvals before activity starts.

9. Intellectual property and site materials

You retain rights in materials you create, subject to any separate written agreement and third-party rights. By submitting information, you permit TARVIKA to use it only as reasonably necessary to review the enquiry, communicate with you and administer the related service. Do not submit material you do not have permission to share.

Website text, branding and other TARVIKA materials may not be copied, republished or commercially used without permission, except where allowed by law.

10. Changes, service availability and contact

Website content, program availability and these terms may be updated. The version in effect when you register or pay, together with any specific program terms presented to you, should be retained for reference. Material changes to a paid program will be communicated to affected learners where practicable.

Questions, complaints, cancellation or refund requests can be sent to info@tarvikart.com or addressed to TARVIKA Research and Technology, 207, Fortune 80, University Road, Udaipur, Rajasthan 323001, India.
