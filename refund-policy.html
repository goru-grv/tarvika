Fees and cancellations

Refund Policy

This policy explains how to request cancellation and a refund for TARVIKA program registration and course fees.

Check the program fee and terms before paying. Program charges can vary. The applicable registration fee, course fee, and any other charge must be shown before payment. This policy is subject to applicable law and any more specific written terms provided for your program.

1. Registration fees

The program registration fee is disclosed before payment and may vary by program. As stated in TARVIKA's Terms & Conditions, the registration fee is non-refundable except where a refund is required by applicable law or TARVIKA confirms otherwise in writing.

2. Course-fee cancellation window

If you decide not to continue, send a written cancellation request to info@tarvikart.com within 7 calendar days after completing course registration or payment. The course fee paid is eligible for a refund under this policy. The separately disclosed registration fee is excluded.

After the 7-calendar-day window, course fees are non-refundable except where a refund is required by applicable law or TARVIKA confirms otherwise in writing.

3. How to submit a request

Email the request from your registered email address and include:

  • Your full name and registered email address.
  • The program name and, if available, your registration reference.
  • The payment date, amount, and transaction or payment reference.
  • A short statement that you are cancelling and requesting a refund of the course fee.

Please do not email passwords, full card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information. TARVIKA may ask for reasonable details needed to verify the registration and payment.

4. Review and processing

TARVIKA will confirm receipt, review the request against the program terms and payment record, and communicate the decision and next steps. If approved, the refund will be sent through the available payment route. The time for funds to appear can depend on the payment provider or bank; TARVIKA will share any processing information available.

Any taxes, gateway charges, materials, or third-party costs will be treated according to the terms disclosed before payment and applicable law. No unlisted charge should be assumed.

5. If TARVIKA cancels a program

If TARVIKA cancels or materially changes a paid program, affected learners will be contacted with the available options and any applicable remedy, including a refund where applicable. The details will depend on the circumstances and the terms communicated for that program.

6. Questions or complaints

For a cancellation, refund request, or concern about a payment, contact info@tarvikart.com. Include your payment reference so the team can locate the transaction. You may also review the full Terms & Conditions.

Last updated: October 2, 2026. This page summarizes TARVIKA's current published refund terms and is a general operational policy. Applicable law and the terms disclosed for a specific transaction continue to apply.
