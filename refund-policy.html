Refund Policy

This policy explains how to request cancellation and a refund for TARVIKA program registration and course fees.

Check the program fee and terms before paying. Program charges can vary. The applicable registration fee, course fee, and any other charge must be shown before payment. This policy is subject to applicable law and any more specific written terms provided for your program.

1. Refund Policy

Customers may request a refund within 10 days of registration or payment, provided the service has not been substantially used or completed. Refund requests submitted after the 10-day period will generally not be eligible for a refund.

2. Registration Fee

The registration fee paid at the time of registration is strictly non-refundable under all circumstances. No refund will be provided for the registration fee, even if the customer cancels the service or requests a refund within the 10-day refund period.

3. How to submit a request

Email the request from your registered email address and include:

  • Your full name and registered email address.
  • The program name and, if available, your registration reference.
  • The payment date, amount, and transaction or payment reference.
  • A short statement that you are cancelling and requesting a refund of the course fee.

Please do not email passwords, full card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information. TARVIKA may ask for reasonable details needed to verify the registration and payment.

4. Service Cancellation

If you wish to cancel a service within the 10-day refund period, you may submit a refund request through our support team. Refund eligibility will depend on the amount of work or service already delivered.

5. Non-Refundable Services

Any payments made to third-party platforms, advertising networks, software providers, hosting providers, or other external services are non-refundable once paid or utilized.

6. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact our support team within **10 days of your registration/payment** and provide your registered details and payment information.

7. Policy Updates

We reserve the right to modify or update this Refund Policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact Us

For refund-related questions or assistance, please contact our support team using the contact details provided on our website.
