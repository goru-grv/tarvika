01. About TARVIKA

What is TARVIKA Research and Technology?

TARVIKA Research and Technology is an Indian research, education, and technology-focused institution located in Udaipur, Rajasthan. We connect Vedic wisdom with modern technology and applied learning, creating a collaborative platform where learners, researchers, and professionals investigate real-world challenges.

Why was TARVIKA started?

TARVIKA was created because potential deserves a place to grow. New fields such as AI, cybersecurity, space technology, and digital agriculture are reshaping how we solve problems. However, knowing about a technology and knowing how to apply it are not the same thing.

TARVIKA closes that distance by offering a space to ask better questions, gain hands-on experience, test ideas, and connect knowledge with useful work, while remaining open to insight from every direction.

What is TARVIKA's core vision and mission?

Vision: Build an ecosystem for thoughtful innovation where traditional knowledge, scientific thinking, and emerging technologies inform one another.

Mission: Turn learning into capability and action by making technology education practical, research-minded, and connected to real challenges.

Our guiding motto is "Research. Technology. Innovation. Rooted in Bharat. Open to the world. Focused on what we can build together."

Does TARVIKA award university degrees?

No. TARVIKA does not award university degrees. TARVIKA offers technology courses, practical training, workshops, guided project learning, and research-oriented initiatives.

Any certificate, recognition, or academic credit associated with a specific offering names the issuing organization and the exact terms that apply to that program.

02. Programs & Disciplines

What programs are currently offered at TARVIKA?

TARVIKA offers emerging technology and practical disciplines, including:

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Intelligent systems, data modeling, neural networks, and responsible AI.
  • Agri Tech: Agriculture connected with IoT sensors, analytics, automation, and digital farming tools.
  • Space Technology: Satellite systems, orbital mechanics, remote sensing, and space payload concepts.
  • Cyber Security: Network security, threat detection, cryptography, and defense architectures.
  • Web Development & DevOps: Modern web application development, deployment pipelines, and cloud workflows.
  • Digital Forensics: Identification, preservation, and analysis of digital evidence and cyber artifacts.
  • Digital Marketing: Strategic brand growth, audience analytics, SEO, and digital media channels.
How are program structures, prerequisites, fees, and durations decided?

Program structures, prerequisites, duration, delivery mode (online/onsite), and fee structures are tailored for each specific cohort and shared by the TARVIKA admissions team. Please contact us directly for current cohort details.

What makes TARVIKA's learning methodology unique?

We follow a three-step learning approach designed around clarity and application:

  • 1. Understand: Define the context, questions, audience, prior knowledge, and objectives.
  • 2. Explore: Study foundational concepts, choose appropriate tools, and experiment with hands-on projects.
  • 3. Reflect: Document findings, evaluate constraints, and identify responsible next steps.

03. Admissions & Registration

Who can apply for TARVIKA programs?

TARVIKA welcomes learners from diverse starting points: students, working professionals, educators, researchers, and domain enthusiasts who are eager to build practical technology capability and research-minded habits.

How do I register or apply for admission?

You can submit an online registration through our Admissions Portal or fill out the enquiry form on our Contact Page. Our admissions team will get in touch with detailed program information and next steps.

Will I receive a certificate upon completion?

Yes. Participants who satisfy program requirements, attendance, and project evaluations receive a Certificate of Completion from TARVIKA Research and Technology detailing the skill areas and project milestones completed.

04. Research Collaboration & Mentorship

How can institutions, researchers, or startups collaborate with TARVIKA?

We welcome academic and industry collaboration on joint research proposals, workshops, guided project briefs, and technical exchange. Submissions can be made via our Research Collaboration Page.

Note: A conversation or proposal is not confirmation of a partnership. All collaborations, scopes, timelines, and responsibilities are formally agreed upon separately with relevant parties.

How does the Mentorship Program work?

TARVIKA connects experienced industry practitioners, educators, and domain experts with learners for guidance on projects, career paths, and technical research questions. Visit our Mentorship Page to learn more or apply as a mentor/mentee.

What responsible research practices does TARVIKA uphold?

We emphasize ethical inquiry, data privacy, participant safety, transparent limitations, and appropriate permissions. Research involving human subjects, sensitive dataset access, or regulated domains undergoes appropriate review before proceeding.
