Marketing Policy

Our marketing should help people make informed decisions about TARVIKA's learning, research, and technology activities.

Clear information comes first. Program availability, fees, schedules, instructors, and outcomes can vary. Confirm current details in the written program information before registering or paying.

1. Accurate and clear claims

TARVIKA aims to describe programs, services, partnerships, and opportunities accurately. Promotional materials should distinguish confirmed details from plans or examples, explain important limits, and avoid claims that could mislead a prospective learner.

  • We do not promise employment, placement, salary, internship, research publication, admission, or a particular business or technical result.
  • We identify program-specific requirements, costs, duration, delivery mode, and certificate availability where those details are confirmed.
  • We do not present a training certificate or recommendation letter as a university degree, professional licence, or accreditation.
  • We correct or remove material promotional errors when they are identified.

2. Testimonials, images, and endorsements

We use a person's name, image, quotation, project, or endorsement in promotional material only with appropriate permission. Testimonials should reflect the person's experience and should not be presented as a guarantee that others will obtain the same result. Any material relationship relevant to an endorsement should be made clear where appropriate.

Images and examples should be used with permission or under a valid licence. We avoid implying that a person, organization, institution, or public body endorses TARVIKA when it has not done so.

3. Promotional messages and preferences

When you submit an enquiry, register for an event, or otherwise ask to receive information, we may use the contact details you provide to respond and send related updates. We aim to keep promotional messages relevant to the request and to identify TARVIKA as the sender.

You can ask to stop promotional email by replying with an opt-out request or writing to info@tarvikart.com. We may still send necessary service messages about an application, payment, or program you use.

4. Personal information and third-party platforms

We use enquiry and registration information for the purposes explained when it is collected, such as answering questions or administering a program. See our Terms & Conditions for more information about information submitted through this website. We do not ask people to send passwords or unnecessary sensitive details in response to a marketing message.

If we share content through a social network, advertising service, or other third-party platform, that platform may process information under its own terms and privacy notice. Please review those notices before interacting with the platform.

5. Questions or corrections

If you believe a TARVIKA promotional message is inaccurate, misleading, or was sent contrary to your communication preference, contact info@tarvikart.com. Include a copy or description of the material so we can review it.

Marketing practices are intended to follow applicable requirements, including consumer-protection rules and guidance. The Government of India's Department of Consumer Affairs publishes the consumer-protection rules and guidelines.

Last updated: October 2, 2026. This policy applies to TARVIKA's promotional communications and materials and does not replace the terms of a specific program or third-party platform.
