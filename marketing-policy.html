Marketing Policy

Our marketing should help people make informed decisions about TARVIKA's learning, research, and technology activities.

Clear information comes first. Program availability, fees, schedules, instructors, and outcomes can vary. Confirm current details in the written program information before registering or paying.

1. Purpose

TARVIKA Research and Technology is committed to communicating its programs, learning opportunities, services and educational initiatives in a clear, responsible and transparent manner. Our marketing activities are designed to help students, learners, professionals and other interested individuals understand what TARVIKA offers and make informed decisions about their learning and career development.

2. Our Marketing Principles

We believe effective marketing should be based on:

  • Transparency — Information about programs, services and opportunities should be presented clearly.
  • Accuracy — Marketing communication should avoid misleading or unverified claims.
  • Value-based communication — Our content focuses on learning, practical skills, projects and technology awareness.
  • Responsible communication — We aim to communicate respectfully and professionally with our audience.
  • Student-focused information — Our marketing should help learners understand available opportunities and make informed choices.

3. Marketing Communications

TARVIKA may communicate information about its programs, workshops, learning resources, events, projects, research activities, collaborations and other relevant initiatives through channels such as:

  • Website and blog
  • Social media platforms
  • Email communication
  • Digital advertising
  • Educational campaigns
  • Events and workshops
  • Promotional materials
  • Direct communication with interested learners

Our website and educational content focus on technology, learning, career development and practical application across areas such as AI & Machine Learning, Web Development & DevOps, Cyber Security, Digital Marketing, Space Technology, Agri Tech and Digital Forensics.

4.Advertising and Promotional Content

Where TARVIKA uses advertising or promotional campaigns, we aim to ensure that the information presented is relevant to the program or opportunity being promoted.

We do not intend to use marketing communication to create false expectations regarding:

  • Employment or placement outcomes
  • Guaranteed career results
  • Guaranteed admission
  • Individual academic performance
  • Professional success
  • Specific outcomes from completing a course or program

Individual results may vary depending on a learner's background, effort, skills, experience and other circumstances.

5.Educational Content

TARVIKA's marketing and blog content may include educational information, technology insights, career guidance and practical learning topics.

Our approach emphasizes learning through practice, projects, documentation and continuous improvement.

Educational content is intended for general informational and learning purposes and should not automatically be considered professional, legal, financial, technical or career advice.

6.Digital Marketing

TARVIKA may use digital marketing methods to reach relevant audiences and communicate information about its programs and initiatives.

These may include:

  • Search engine marketing
  • Social media marketing
  • Content marketing
  • Email campaigns
  • Online advertisements
  • Website analytics
  • Campaign performance analysis

7.Privacy and Personal Information

TARVIKA respects the privacy of individuals who interact with its website, campaigns and communication channels.

Personal information collected through forms, registrations, enquiries or other communication channels should be handled responsibly and used for legitimate purposes such as responding to enquiries, providing requested information, managing registrations or communicating relevant updates.

Marketing communication should not intentionally misuse personal information or use deceptive practices.

8.Responsible Marketing

TARVIKA aims to avoid marketing practices that are:

  • Misleading or deceptive
  • Discriminatory or disrespectful
  • Designed to create unnecessary pressure
  • Based on false claims
  • Intended to exploit a learner's lack of technical knowledge
  • Inconsistent with the information provided about a program or service
