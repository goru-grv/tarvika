Cyber Security

Learn how to reason about digital risk and strengthen systems through authorized, defensive practice. Security work begins with permission, scope, careful documentation and respect for people and data.

Register your interest ↗All programs
What this field explores

Protect systems by understanding how they can fail.

Cyber security concerns the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information and services. It combines technical controls with risk management, user awareness, secure design and incident readiness.

Learning should happen in systems you own or have explicit permission to assess. TARVIKA's possible study path emphasizes defensive principles and safe lab environments; any specific practical activity and scope must be confirmed for the course.

Core lensRisk, systems, networks and defensive controls
Learning modeConcepts, controlled labs and documented analysis
Responsible focusWritten authorization, privacy and safe disclosure
Possible areas of study

Build a defender's understanding.

Exact depth and tools depend on the specific learning program.

01 / FOUNDATIONS

Security principles and risk

Explore threat, vulnerability, impact, likelihood and how controls reduce risk.

02 / SYSTEMS

Operating systems and networks

Understand basic system components, network concepts, services and trust boundaries.

03 / IDENTITY

Accounts and access

Study authentication, authorization, least privilege, account hygiene and access review.

04 / PROTECTION

Secure configurations and data

Consider updates, backups, encryption concepts, hardening and data handling.

05 / RESPONSE

Monitoring and incident readiness

Learn how logs, escalation plans, evidence preservation and clear reporting support response.

06 / GOVERNANCE

Policy, privacy and disclosure

Discuss authorization, responsible disclosure, legal context and protection of affected users.

Research and project ideas

Practice defense in a defined, authorized scope.

Projects must use owned or explicitly authorized systems, test data and isolated labs.

PROJECT IDEA 01

Secure configuration review

Use a provided lab checklist to identify configuration gaps in a deliberately designed practice environment.

PROJECT IDEA 02

Small-organization threat model

Map assets, trust boundaries and mitigations for a fictional or consented low-risk scenario.

PROJECT IDEA 03

Incident response tabletop

Prepare roles, communication and evidence-handling steps for a simulated incident.

A possible learner roadmap

Move from fundamentals to a defensible response.

01 / MAP

Understand the environment

Identify assets, users, boundaries, permissions and potential impact.

02 / ASSESS

Reason about risk

Use an approved framework or checklist to organize observations.

03 / DEFEND

Apply safe controls

Practice hardening, access hygiene, backups or response planning in a lab.

04 / REPORT

Communicate responsibly

Document scope, findings, limitations and remediation in clear language.

Who may find it useful

Learners curious about secure computing, networks, digital safety, governance or defensive operations.

What to bring

Patience, respect for boundaries, attention to detail and readiness to follow written scope.

What you may develop

Security vocabulary, basic defensive analysis, risk communication and documented lab work.

What is not allowed or promised

No unauthorized testing, real-world intrusion or unapproved data access. No job, certification or degree is guaranteed; TARVIKA does not award university degrees.

Never test a public website, network, account or device without explicit written authorization and a defined scope. Use only designated labs and systems.
