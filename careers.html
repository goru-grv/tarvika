A practical 4-step plan

Turn “I want to work in tech” into a next step.

Keep the plan small enough to revisit. Your direction can change as you learn more.

  1. 01

    Name a role to explore

    Write down one role title and two reasons it interests you. Save a few current job descriptions to compare requirements.

  2. 02

    Choose one skill gap

    Pick a specific skill from those descriptions. Find a credible learning source and set a realistic practice goal.

  3. 03

    Make a small proof of work

    Create a project, analysis or case study that demonstrates the skill. Use only data and materials you are permitted to share.

  4. 04

    Ask for feedback and iterate

    Request specific feedback, improve your explanation, then apply selectively to roles that fit your current preparation.
