About TARVIKA

Research-minded learning for meaningful technology.

TARVIKA Research and Technology is a research, education and technology-focused institution in Udaipur, Rajasthan. We bring practical learning, applied research and open collaboration together, rooted in Bharat and engaged with a changing world.

आ नो भद्राः क्रतवो यन्तु विश्वतः।

Who we are

A place to learn, investigate and build.

Technology changes quickly. We believe people need more than access to tools: they need sound foundations, room to test ideas, responsible guidance and a community that values useful questions.

OUR PURPOSE

Connect knowledge to real work.

TARVIKA creates a platform for students, researchers, professionals, startups and organizations to explore emerging technology, learn through practice and investigate challenges with care. We aim to connect academic concepts with prototypes, experiments and applications that can be understood, tested and improved.

OUR ROOTS

Open inquiry, grounded in Bharat.

Our name and outlook reflect a respect for India's intellectual traditions and an openness to knowledge from every direction. The Vedic invocation “आ नो भद्राः क्रतवो यन्तु विश्वतः” expresses that spirit: welcome good ideas from all sides, then examine them through thoughtful, evidence-aware inquiry.

Our direction

Vision & Mission

A shared direction for learners, researchers, professionals and collaborators.

01 / VISION

An ecosystem for thoughtful innovation.

We envision a modern research and technology ecosystem where learners, researchers, professionals, startups and organizations can learn, experiment and collaborate. We want traditional knowledge, scientific thinking and emerging technologies to inform one another, creating a culture that welcomes questions and tests ideas with care.

Our long-term aspiration is a community that can move between learning, research and responsible application, while remaining curious about perspectives and discoveries from across India and the world.

02 / MISSION

Turn learning into capability and action.

Our mission is to make technology education practical, research-minded and connected to real challenges. Through courses, workshops, guided projects and research initiatives, we aim to help participants develop useful skills, responsible habits and the confidence to keep learning throughout their careers.

We encourage clear objectives, honest evaluation, documentation and collaboration so that ideas can be discussed, tested and developed with appropriate context.

What we do

Learning and inquiry across emerging fields.

Our activities are designed around technology education, practical exploration and applied research. Exact modules, duration, delivery and eligibility are shared for each specific program.

TECHNOLOGY LEARNING

Courses, workshops and guided practice.

Explore areas including Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning, Digital Marketing, Agri Tech, Space Technology, Cyber Security, Web Development and DevOps, and Digital Forensics through structured learning and practical exercises.

APPLIED RESEARCH

Projects, prototypes and experiments.

Develop research questions, examine relevant context, plan a suitable approach and document observations. Project scope depends on the topic, available expertise, resources, approvals and agreed responsibilities.

COLLABORATION

Exchange ideas across communities.

We welcome conversations with learners, mentors, academic institutions, industry practitioners and community organizations about workshops, project briefs, research dialogue and other mutually agreed activities.

RESPONSIBLE PRACTICE

Consider people, evidence and impact.

We encourage clear consent, privacy, safety, appropriate permissions and transparent limits in project work. Research involving people, sensitive information or regulated settings requires suitable review and approvals before it proceeds.

How we work

From question to considered next step.

Our approach connects foundations with exploration and keeps the purpose of each activity visible.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Set the context

Define the question, audience, prior knowledge, constraints and goals.

02 / EXPLORE

Learn and test

Study the foundations, choose appropriate tools and experiment with care.

03 / REFLECT

Document and improve

Review evidence, describe limitations and identify responsible next steps.

Not every idea becomes a formal research project or partnership. Scope, participation, deliverables and any fees are discussed and agreed before work begins.

INSTITUTIONAL SCOPE

Research- and learning-oriented.

TARVIKA offers technology courses, training, workshops, project learning and research-oriented initiatives. TARVIKA does not award university degrees. Any certificate, recognition or academic credit must name the issuing organization and the terms that apply to that specific program.

COLLABORATION STATUS

A conversation or proposal is not confirmation of a partnership, funding, publication, mentorship or acceptance. Any collaboration, fees, deliverables, timeline and responsibilities are confirmed separately with the relevant parties.

Program availability, instructors, fees, schedules and eligibility can change. Please contact TARVIKA for the current details of a specific offering before making a decision.
