A simple way to begin

Make your reading active.

Use a small, repeatable loop to turn resources into understanding.

01 / CHOOSE

Pick one question

Write down what you want to understand and what you already know.

02 / STUDY

Use a primary source

Start with official documentation or a named research source; note the version and date you consulted.

03 / TRY

Build a tiny exercise

Apply one idea to a small example using data and systems you are allowed to use.

04 / REFLECT

Record what changed

Note what worked, what failed, what surprised you and what you would check next.
