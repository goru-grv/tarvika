What to include

Send the details below so candidates can understand the role before applying:

  • Employer name and a verifiable organization website or profile
  • Role title, responsibilities, required skills, and preferred experience
  • Location, work mode, role type, expected hours, and duration
  • Compensation or stipend range, payment terms, and benefits if applicable
  • Supervisor or team context and any training or equipment provided
  • Application destination, contact, and deadline
  • Any eligibility, travel, language, or accessibility requirements

Unpaid roles must be labeled clearly and include duration, expected hours, supervision, learning or project scope, and the reason the role is unpaid.

Review and publication process

  1. Send: Email the role details using the submission checklist below.
  2. Review: TARVIKA checks completeness, relevance, and clarity; additional verification may be requested.
  3. Confirm: Agree the listing text, fee if any, publication period, and closing date before it goes live.
  4. Publish: Approved listings display the date checked and employer application route.
  5. Close: Notify TARVIKA when a role is filled, withdrawn, or changed.

Submission does not guarantee publication. Employers remain responsible for accurate details and lawful hiring practices.

Email submission checklist: Attach or paste the role description and include your name, organization, website, preferred publication dates, and a contact who can verify the role.
