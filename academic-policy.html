Learning standards and learner support

Academic Policy

This policy sets out the learning and participation principles that guide TARVIKA's training and research-oriented programs.

Program details vary. The syllabus, eligibility, schedule, attendance expectations, assessments, and completion requirements are specific to each program and will be communicated before enrolment where they apply.

1. Scope and status of programs

TARVIKA offers skill training, workshops, and research-oriented learning activities. These activities are not university degree programs. A course certificate or recommendation letter, if a particular program offers one, is not a university degree, professional licence, or guarantee of admission or employment. See the Terms & Conditions for related disclosures.

2. Program information and admission

Before applying or enrolling, review the current program description for its learning objectives, prerequisites, format, schedule, duration, fee, available support, and any assessment or completion requirements. Ask TARVIKA to clarify any material detail that is missing before paying.

Applicants should provide complete and accurate information and submit only genuine documents they are authorized to share. Admission or a place in a program is confirmed only through communication from TARVIKA. Enquiry submission alone does not guarantee acceptance or a seat.

3. Participation and learning environment

Learners are expected to participate respectfully, follow program instructions, meet communicated deadlines, and use equipment, data, and online spaces responsibly. Harassment, discrimination, threats, disruption, unauthorized access, or misuse of another person's work or information is not acceptable.

For project and research work, learners should keep appropriate notes, credit sources, protect private or confidential information, and obtain permission before collecting or sharing data. Security exercises must stay within a written authorized scope.

4. Academic integrity and use of tools

Submit work that reflects your own contribution. Clearly identify quotations, references, collaborators, reused material, and any assistance that a program requires you to disclose. Do not fabricate results, falsify records, plagiarize, or present another person's work as your own.

Use of generative AI or other tools must follow the instructions for the specific activity. Where disclosure is required, describe what tool was used and how it contributed. Learners remain responsible for checking accuracy, permissions, privacy, and citations in submitted work.

5. Attendance, assessment, and completion

Attendance, assessment methods, grading (if any), and completion criteria are set out for each program. Where a program has formal requirements, TARVIKA will explain them before the relevant activity. Learners should contact the program team promptly if illness, accessibility needs, or another serious circumstance affects participation.

Completion documents are issued only if the program offers them and the stated criteria are met. A certificate is not automatic and should not be described as a degree or professional credential.

6. Respectful conduct and support

We aim to provide a respectful learning environment. Learners can raise accessibility, safety, or participation concerns with the program contact and may request reasonable adjustments by explaining what support would help. Requests should be made as early as practicable so the team can discuss available options.

Concerns will be handled discreetly where practicable and shared only with people who need the information to review or respond. Immediate safety concerns should be reported promptly.

7. Concerns and review requests

For a concern about a program decision, assessment, conduct issue, or learning support, write to info@tarvikart.com. Include your name, program, the decision or issue you want reviewed, and relevant dates or records. Do not include passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

TARVIKA will acknowledge the concern and explain the review process or next steps. Program-specific rules may describe additional review routes or timelines.

8. Changes and records

Program materials, schedules, and learning arrangements may need to change. TARVIKA will communicate material changes to affected learners where practicable. Learner information is handled as described in the Terms & Conditions and relevant program notices.

Last updated: October 2, 2026. Program-specific written information supplements this general policy. TARVIKA does not award university degrees.
