Mentorship & guidance

Learn with direction. Grow through reflection.

Good mentorship can help turn a broad ambition into a useful question, a practical learning plan and considered next steps.

A supportive learning culture

Guidance that helps you take ownership.

Mentorship is a conversation and feedback process, not a substitute for your own practice or a promise of career outcomes.

01 / DIRECTION

Clarify what you want to learn.

Discuss interests, current foundations and a realistic goal so the next learning step feels focused and achievable.

02 / FEEDBACK

Improve work through review.

Where available, project feedback can help learners explain decisions, spot gaps and plan iterations.

03 / PERSPECTIVE

Hear from people in the field.

Sessions with practitioners or researchers may offer context about tools, workflows, questions and professional practice.

04 / NEXT STEPS

Make informed choices.

Use discussion and reflection to consider further learning, project ideas, research directions or career exploration.

How a guidance conversation can work

Focused, practical and respectful.

01 / PREPARE

Bring a question

Share what you are working on, what you have tried and where you feel stuck.

02 / EXPLORE

Discuss possibilities

Consider approaches, resources and trade-offs without assuming one path fits everyone.

03 / REFLECT

Choose an action

Leave with a manageable next step and continue building independence through practice.

Mentor availability, session format, eligibility and any associated fees depend on the specific program. A mentor match, job referral, placement or particular result is not guaranteed. Please contact TARVIKA to ask what is currently available.
