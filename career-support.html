Career support

Career support for your next step

A career path is built through learning, practice, feedback, and informed choices. TARVIKA’s career support is designed to help learners and early-career professionals identify a useful next step in technology.

Support may include mentorship conversations, resume and portfolio feedback, interview practice, or guidance toward relevant training. Availability, format, fees, and eligibility depend on the specific service and current capacity. No service guarantees employment, placement, certification, or a particular career outcome.

01 · Mentorship

Talk through your goals with a mentor

Discuss your goals, current skills, and questions with a mentor where a suitable match is available. A conversation can help identify priorities, plan a project, or explore possible pathways in AI, cybersecurity, software, Agri Tech, or digital marketing. Mentorship depends on mentor availability and mutual agreement. Confirm format, scope, schedule, and any cost before beginning.

Ask about mentorship
02 · Resume and portfolio

Make your experience easier to understand

Get feedback on how clearly your experience, projects, and skills are presented. A review may focus on structure, relevance to a role, project descriptions, and evidence of your contribution. Bring a target role or program if you have one. Feedback is educational guidance; you remain responsible for the accuracy of your application and following employer instructions.

Request a resume review
03 · Interview preparation

Practise explaining your work

Prepare to explain projects, respond to role-related questions, and ask an employer informed questions. Preparation can include a mock interview, a review of your project narrative, or a plan for technical practice. Format and availability are confirmed before scheduling. Interview preparation does not represent an employer endorsement or guarantee of selection.

Enquire about interview practice
04 · Training

Build the skills you need next

Explore relevant TARVIKA learning programs and resources in AI and machine learning, cybersecurity, web development and DevOps, Agri Tech, space technology, digital forensics, and digital marketing. Curriculum, schedule, delivery format, fees, prerequisites, and certificate terms vary. TARVIKA does not award university degrees.
